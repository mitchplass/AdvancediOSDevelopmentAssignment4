# RideReady support

Email [mitchellplass@gmail.com](mailto:mitchellplass@gmail.com?subject=RideReady%20support) with questions about a check, a ride, or your data. RideReady needs iOS 26.2 or later.

## Set up your bike

Open the Bike tab and tap Edit. Enter the name, final drive (chain, shaft, or belt), tyre pressures, and chain slack. The pre-ride check uses these numbers, and a chain bike gets a chain check while shaft and belt bikes get a final-drive check.

## Do a pre-ride check

On the Bike tab, tap Start pre-ride check. Mark each item OK or Issue, then log the check. The Bike tab shows whether you are ride ready, and your streak counts the days in a row you logged a check.

## Break reminders don't arrive

Break reminders are notifications on this iPhone. If you said no when iOS asked, open Settings, then Notifications, then RideReady, and turn on Allow Notifications. The Ride tab shows an Open Settings button when they are off. You can set the reminder from every 30 minutes to every 4 hours.

## Add the widget

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget. Search for RideReady and choose the small or medium Pre-ride check widget. It shows your bike, when you last checked it, and anything that needs attention.

## Record a track day

Open the Lap Timer tab. Register a track first, then tap the stopwatch to record a session with the weather, track condition, setup notes, and lap times. The session view shows your fastest lap, average, and consistency.

## Erase your data

Open the Appearance tab and tap Erase ride data. This deletes the bike, photo, checks, rides, journal, lap times, streak, and reminder settings from this iPhone. Deleting the app removes everything RideReady stored.

## Privacy

RideReady has no account and keeps everything on this iPhone. Read the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).
